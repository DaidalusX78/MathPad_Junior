2. Exploring School-Themed Play Formats & Time Structures
Linking the gameplay, time limits, and session structures to the authentic rhythm of a School Day & Academic Calendar creates an intuitive, playful learning metaphor. Here are 3 distinct architectural options for structuring this:

Option A: "The School Day" — Daily Session Modes & Speed Challenges
Organize the play format around the daily school bell and routine:

Period / Mode	Format & Timing	Gameplay Purpose & Reward
🔔 Morning Registration	3–5 Quick warm-up questions	Quick daily check-in to preserve and extend the Daily Streak 🔥.
📚 Lesson Time (Classes)	10–15 Standard questions	Focused single-topic curriculum practice (e.g. Class 3A: Long Multiplication).
🏃 Morning Break (Recess)	60-second speed dash	Rapid-fire mental math sprint. Answer as many as possible for bonus coins.
🥪 Lunchtime Club	1 Daily riddle / word problem	Multi-step real-world puzzle offering a star chest upon completion.
🎨 After-School Club	Open sandbox	Free-play exploration with no timer, custom paper textures, and doodle pad.
🚨 Detention (Mistake Buster!)	3–5 Targeted correction problems	A playful recovery mode: When a student gets questions wrong during a lesson, they can enter "Detention" to solve those exact problems correctly and earn their lost stars back!
Option B: "The Academic Calendar" — Long-Term Seasons, Terms & Holidays
Structure progression into natural school terms with themed events and graduations:

School Terms (Trimesters / Progression Seasons):
Autumn Term: Foundations & Speed (Addition, Subtraction, 2x–5x tables).
Spring Term: Structures & Shapes (Multiplication, Division, 6x–12x tables, Geometry).
Summer Term: Mastery & Real-World Math (Multi-step Word Problems, Decimals, Fractions).
Graduation / Year-End Exams: A celebratory 20-question challenge at the end of each term awarding an exclusive End of Term Trophy and diploma certificate.
School Holidays (Seasonal Special Events):
Half-Term Camp / Summer Holidays / Winter Break: Special limited-time event boards where students can unlock holiday-exclusive stationery (e.g., Halloween Ghost Ink, Winter Snowflake Palm Mat, Summer Beach Doodles).
School Year Levels (Class 1 to Class 6):
Instead of generic levels (Level 1, Level 2), students advance from Reception / Year 1 up to Year 6 / Graduation as their total questions answered and mastery increase.
Option C: "The School Timetable" — Visual Schedule & Homework Planner
Transform the Home Screen / Practice Setup into an interactive School Timetable Grid:

Monday to Friday Timetable:
Each day displays 4–5 clickable "Periods" on a school planner board.
Period 1: Mental Maths ➡️ Period 2: Times Tables Gym ➡️ Breaktime Sprint ➡️ Period 3: Geometry Lab.
Completed periods get stamped with a gold star sticker ⭐ or a teacher's checkmark 🟢.
"Golden Time" (Friday Reward):
Completing all periods during the week unlocks Golden Time — a double-stars bonus hour and special shopping discounts in The Pencil Case!
